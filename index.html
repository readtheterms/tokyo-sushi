Our Story

Your neighborhood sushi bar

20+ years serving Redlands

For more than two decades, Tokyo Sushi has been a family-run fixture on Redlands Boulevard. We keep it simple: generous rolls, fresh fish, and a warm welcome, whether it's your first visit or your five-hundredth.

Stop in for a lunch bento on your break, or settle in at the bar for a round of specialty rolls. Pull up a seat.
